How this plan works

Savings grow monthly, with contributions at month-end. Retirement applies growth, income and withdrawals monthly until your planning age. Spending and entered pension estimates rise monthly with assumed inflation. Income above spending is not reinvested. A zero balance never becomes debt: unmet spending is reported separately.

CPP/OAS entries are your age-65 monthly estimates in today’s dollars. Start-age factors are applied; OAS increases 10% from age 75. Actual entitlement, QPP, taxes, OAS recovery tax, GIS, fees, RRIF minimums and account withdrawal order are not modeled. Benefits before retirement are assumed spent.

Account room is assessed separately for 2026, not enforced across future projection years. Retirement investments exclude FHSA, home cash and emergency cash. Targets assume fixed returns, no estate target and the selected spending horizon. They are not probabilities or guarantees.
